My Journey in Computer Science

My foundation started in competitive programming and core computer science—writing C and C++ to master data structures, graph algorithms, and object-oriented design. Over my undergraduate years at Netrokona University, that algorithmic curiosity expanded into building complete software systems and conducting deep learning research.

Currently in my 4th year (1st semester), my research focuses on Computer Vision and Synthetic Media Detection, while my engineering work spans Django web platforms, LLM-powered Streamlit tools, and native Java Android applications. Beyond academics, I served as the General Secretary of the founding committee of the Computer Club and as the elected Class Representative (2021–22 batch)—roles where I learned to lead initiatives, organize technical events, and thrive in collaborative environments.
